Find the relevant FAO 233 pathway

§ 22.1 Filipino researcher • no foreign funding

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.1. Baseline processing: BFAR Central Office under Section 22.7; see exceptions below.

  • Letter of application
  • Non-refundable application fee
  • Personal résumé
  • Brief research description or project proposal
  • Endorsement from the head of the affiliated institution; for an independent researcher, endorsement by a recognized expert from a research/academic institution or conservation organization
  • Site-appropriate prior consent or clearance: Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC, or concerned LGU clearance in consultation with FARMC; PAMB for protected areas, relevant rights holders and other agencies as applicable. Confirm which applies to each site; these are not interchangeable blanket clearances.
  • Affidavit of Undertaking; MOA with the Secretary instead where the specified toxin/pharmacological/commercial-property condition applies
  • GP issued by the Secretary if collection is from the wild, after completing requirements
§ 22.2 Foreign researcher, affiliation or funding

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.2. Baseline processing: BFAR Central Office under Section 22.7; see exceptions below.

  • Letter of application
  • Institutional profile or personal résumé
  • Brief research description or project proposal
  • Disclosure of the source of research funds
  • Local institution as research collaborator/counterpart and consent letter from its head
  • Site-appropriate prior consent or clearance: Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC, or concerned LGU clearance in consultation with FARMC; PAMB for protected areas, relevant rights holders and other agencies as applicable. Confirm which applies to each site; these are not interchangeable blanket clearances.
  • Non-refundable application fee
  • MOA between the Secretary and the proponent
  • GP issued by the Secretary for wild collection after completing requirements
§ 22.3 Conservation breeding or wildlife conservation project

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.3. Baseline processing: BFAR Central Office under Section 22.7; see exceptions below.

  • Letter of intent
  • Research/project proposal
  • Habitat restoration, protection and management plan for reintroduction/restocking of captive-bred individuals (captive-breeding projects only)
  • Acceptance letter from local collaborators (foreign applicants only)
  • Institutional or organizational profile
  • Endorsement from the institution/organization head
  • Disclosure of project funding source
  • Prior clearance under Rule 7.4 of Joint DA–DENR–PCSD AO No. 1; confirm site-specific authorities
  • Non-refundable application and processing fees
§ 22.4 Government collection for certified urgent concerns

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.4. Conditional exemption from the scientific-research rule; confirm eligibility with BFAR.

  • Certification by the government agency that collection addresses an urgent concern covered by Section 22.4
  • Prior notice to BFAR
  • Written research results and recommended plan of action to BFAR
§ 22.5 Government-initiated or implemented research

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.5. Baseline processing: Concerned BFAR Regional Office under Section 22.7; see exceptions below.

  • Letter of intent/application
  • Approved Work and Financial Plan
  • Research proposal
  • Site-appropriate prior consent or clearance: Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC, or concerned LGU clearance in consultation with FARMC; PAMB for protected areas, relevant rights holders and other agencies as applicable. Confirm which applies to each site; these are not interchangeable blanket clearances.
  • Grant or other agreement covering research funding
  • GP issued by the Regional Director for wild collection after completing requirements
§ 22.6 Student thesis or dissertation at a local institution

Required by FAO 233 Section 22.6. Baseline processing: Concerned BFAR Regional Office under Section 22.7; see exceptions below.

  • Letter of application
  • Certified true copy of the approved thesis proposal
  • Endorsement letters from the concerned Dean and thesis adviser
  • Site-appropriate prior consent or clearance: Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC, or concerned LGU clearance in consultation with FARMC; PAMB for protected areas, relevant rights holders and other agencies as applicable. Confirm which applies to each site; these are not interchangeable blanket clearances.
  • Non-refundable application fee for graduate students
  • Affidavit of Undertaking; MOA between the Secretary and the student where the specified toxin/pharmacological/commercial-property condition applies
  • GP issued by the Regional Director, or by the Secretary for species covered by MOA, for wild collection after completing requirements
Routing exceptions and current-office confirmation

Section 22.9: research covering two or more regions goes to Central Office. Section 22.7 sends 22.1–22.3 to Central Office and 22.4–22.6 to the concerned Regional Office, subject to the 22.6 MOA exception.

Threatened / protected / CITES: special review required — confirm the applicable permit and processing office with BFAR. Corals: the supplied BFAR 4A operational guidance refers cases to Central Office–FRLD–AWRS. Toxin / pharmacological / commercial-property cases: an MOA/Secretary-level route may apply.

The 2024 BFAR Citizen’s Charter describes some regional processing differently from the original Section 22.7. This guide shows the original FAO baseline and flags current delegation and office routing for confirmation; it does not silently treat the charter as an amendment.

PREPARE WELL

Make your collection plan easy to review.

BFAR 4A practical guidance
Use grams

State collection weight in grams. Add counts where helpful; use consistent quantities across documents.

Allow a realistic date range

Use a flexible proposed range for fieldwork. Collect only within the dates authorized by the issued permit.

Name every collector

Identify all personnel, collaborators and collectors involved, with their roles.

Locate every site

Give site names, municipality/province and coordinates, with species, methods and quantities per site.

The regional Annex A work plan is not the species-list Annex A of FAO 233. Confirm the accepted regional form; the download below is a preparation worksheet.

How to approach prior consent and clearances
Required by FAO 233 · applicable subsections

Site-appropriate prior consent or clearance: Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC, or concerned LGU clearance in consultation with FARMC; PAMB for protected areas, relevant rights holders and other agencies as applicable. Confirm which applies to each site; these are not interchangeable blanket clearances.

Identify each collection site first, then ask BFAR which authority must issue the consent or clearance. Do not substitute a generic LGU letter for Indigenous Peoples’ FPIC or PAMB clearance where applicable.

Common issues to check before sending
  • Scientific names and quantities match across the letter, proposal and work plan.
  • Dean and adviser endorsements are included for the student pathway.
  • Scans are readable; signatures and certifications required for your pathway are present.
  • All sites, collectors, funding sources and special species concerns are disclosed.
  • Use the correct current BFAR format; illustrative downloads are not approved forms.

04 / QUICK ANSWERS

The questions that come up.

Based on the topics preserved in the supplied brief and available chat; not a verbatim record of unavailable applicant conversations.

20 questions

Can I use only the common name?

Include the scientific name if known and verify the identification and regulatory status with BFAR. This guide does not look up species status. An unknown status triggers special review.

Is every researcher under Section 22.5?

No. Section 22.5 concerns government-initiated or implemented research. Filipino researchers without foreign funding may fall under 22.1; foreign affiliation/funding under 22.2; qualifying conservation projects under 22.3. A fee category does not determine the section.

Do threatened or CITES-listed species always go to Central Office?

This guide does not infer a blanket rule from FAO 233. Special review is required: confirm the applicable permit and processing office with BFAR. A GP alone must not be assumed sufficient.

Where do coral applications go?

The BFAR 4A operational guidance supplied for this guide refers coral cases to BFAR Central Office – Fisheries Regulatory and Licensing Division (FRLD) – Aquatic Wildlife Regulatory Section (AWRS). This is regional guidance, not a blanket routing provision quoted from FAO 233.

What if I collect in two or more regions?

Section 22.9 directs multi-region research applications to BFAR Central Office. Multiple sites within one region do not by themselves mean multi-region research.

How much do I pay as a Master’s or Doctoral student?

The supplied BFAR 4A practical total is ₱120: ₱100 research application fee plus ₱20 GP fee. Undergraduate Filipino students: ₱20 total. Other researchers/project-based applicants: generally ₱200 total. Confirm special cases, nationality-related eligibility and exemptions with the processing office.

Can I pay both fees in one transaction?

Yes, the supplied regional workflow allows one transaction for convenience. FAO 233 still distinguishes the research application and GP fee components. Get the Order of Payment first; this website does not take payments.

Should my email contain one PDF or separate files?

Either one combined PDF or separate readable PDFs is acceptable under the supplied BFAR 4A guidance. Send TO region4arecords@bfar.da.gov.ph and CC frms.4a@bfar.da.gov.ph. Check legibility and that every applicable document is attached.

Should Annex A use grams or number of pieces?

The regional work-plan guidance asks for grams. Specify weight in grams; counts may be added for clarity. Identify scientific names, sites/coordinates, methods, all personnel/collectors and a flexible date range. Do not treat the date range as permission to collect before approval.

Is the work-plan Annex A the same as FAO 233 Annex A?

No. “Annex A work plan” here refers to the regional application attachment. Annex A in FAO 233 is a species list. The downloadable work-plan worksheet is a newly drafted aid, not the missing regional original.

Do I need both a Dean and thesis adviser endorsement?

Section 22.6 lists endorsement letters from the concerned Dean and thesis adviser. The illustrative drafts show both roles; ask BFAR about any proposed combined format.

Who should appear in the collection plan?

List all personnel and collectors involved, including collaborators, with their roles. Confirm changes with BFAR and follow the approved permit’s personnel, site, quantity and date conditions.

Can I collect while my application is being processed?

An application or payment is not a permit. Wait for the applicable authorization and follow its conditions. Ask the processing office to confirm your collection schedule.

What if specimens are accidentally collected?

Include accidentally collected biological resources in the Sampling Report and Inventory under the supplied regional guidance. Report issues promptly to BFAR and follow permit conditions; a later inventory does not authorize collection.

When is the Sampling Report and Inventory due?

The supplied regional guidance sets the deadline within 60 days after the last day of collection. Check the issued permit and current BFAR instructions for any additional or earlier obligations.

Where do I send the final thesis or research results?

For the regional workflow, email Records and CC FRMS. Section 22.10 also requires a copy of the research results to BFAR and NFRDI. Confirm NFRDI’s current submission channel; do not assume a Records email fulfills that separate submission.

What happens after submission?

BFAR checks completeness, evaluates the applicable route and may request clarification or revisions. Section 22.8 says incomplete applications shall not be accepted. Coordinate payment and permit release with the processing office. No processing time is promised by this guide.

Does a government grant automatically mean Section 22.5?

No. Confirm whether the research is government-initiated or implemented. Funding alone should not automatically classify a faculty or private project under Section 22.5.

What if my samples are purchased or cultured?

Disclose the actual source and seek BFAR advice on coverage and acquisition/transport documents. The Section 22 GP provisions refer to wild collection; this guide does not declare purchased or cultured material exempt.

What about Palawan, export or commercial product development?

Seek specific advice on jurisdiction and the applicable permits. This regional guide does not decide Palawan/PCSD, export/CITES or bioprospecting requirements. A research GP must not be assumed to cover them.
