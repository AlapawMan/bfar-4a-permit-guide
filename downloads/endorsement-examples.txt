ILLUSTRATIVE ENDORSEMENT DRAFTS — NOT ISSUED OR APPROVED LETTERS
The original uploaded samples were not accessible. The appropriate institution official must review and issue the actual letter; replace brackets and confirm the addressee with BFAR.

EXAMPLE 1 — CONCERNED DEAN (STUDENT THESIS/DISSERTATION)
[Date / official letterhead / confirmed BFAR addressee]
I endorse [student's full name], a [degree/program] student of [institution], in connection with the approved thesis/dissertation proposal entitled [title]. The proposed research involves [species, sites, dates and brief purpose]. Please evaluate the application under the applicable BFAR requirements.
[Dean's name, designation, signature and institutional contact]

EXAMPLE 2 — THESIS ADVISER
[Date / official letterhead / confirmed BFAR addressee]
I serve as thesis adviser to [student] for [approved title]. I endorse the student's application for the proposed collection described in the approved proposal and accompanying work plan: [brief scope].
[Adviser's name, designation, signature and institutional contact]

EXAMPLE 3 — HEAD OF INSTITUTION / ORGANIZATION
[Date / official letterhead / confirmed BFAR addressee]
On behalf of [institution], I endorse [proponent] for [research title], involving [brief scope]. The applicant is affiliated with this institution as [role]. Project funding and collaborators are [details].
[Head's name, designation, signature and institutional contact]

Section 22.6 lists endorsements from the concerned Dean and thesis adviser. Other sections have their own endorsement/collaborator requirements. These examples do not establish that one combined letter is accepted.
