ILLUSTRATIVE REQUESTS FOR CLEARANCE — NOT ACTUAL CLEARANCES
Ask BFAR which authority must issue consent/clearance for each site. A request letter is not approval. These drafts are not FPIC certificates, PAMB resolutions or official LGU-issued forms.

EXAMPLE 1 — REQUEST TO THE CONCERNED LGU
[Date / appropriate LGU official, to be confirmed]
Subject: Request for site clearance for proposed aquatic wildlife research
I am [name/institution], proposing [research title/purpose] at [site, municipality, province, coordinates], during [proposed date range]. Proposed species, quantities in grams, collection methods and all collectors are [details].
Please advise on the applicable clearance process, including consultation with FARMC where required. Attached for review are [proposal/work plan and relevant documents]. Collection will be subject to the applicable authorizations.
[Applicant signature and contact]

EXAMPLE 2 — REQUEST FOR PROTECTED-AREA PROCEDURE GUIDANCE
[Date / appropriate protected-area office/PAMB contact, to be confirmed]
I seek guidance on prior clearance for [project] at [protected-area site]. The planned collection is [species, grams, methods, personnel and dates]. Please confirm the review process and documents required by the appropriate authority.
[Applicant signature and contact]

Indigenous Peoples' FPIC and valid private/tenurial rights may require separate procedures. Do not substitute these request examples for those procedures. Refer to the applicable subsection of FAO 233 Section 22.
